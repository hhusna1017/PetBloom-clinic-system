Our Background

PetBloom Animal Clinic Kuala Lumpur is a dedicated veterinary clinic established in 2020. We are conveniently located at No. 8, Jalan Telawi 3, Bangsar Baru, 59100 Kuala Lumpur, Wilayah Persekutuan Kuala Lumpur.

With a team of highly experienced, passionate, and knowledgeable veterinarians, the clinic stands firm on its foundational promise to ensure that every individual pet under our roof receives proper, attentive, and compassionate care. We pride ourselves on building lasting bonds of trust within our local community.
